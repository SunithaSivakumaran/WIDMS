# Spectacles-only Vision Camps

Install/upgrade using `php database/migrate.php`, then `php database/verify.php`. The new tables are defined exclusively in `migration_spectacle_camps.sql`; no manual database edits are required. Existing `vision_camps`, lens workflows, historic distributions and ordinary inventory quantities are left untouched.

## Role navigation

All four sidebars have a **Vision Camps** group. Subject Officers create requests and manage their own camps. Their **Camp History** page includes every camp, even those requested by other Subject Officers, with read-only access to those camps' details and history. Ownership remains required for participant changes, stock requests, transfers, distributions and completion. Admin reviews camp and stock requests using the same card component. Store Keeper records camp-specific receipts and releases approved requests. SSOs see only approved/completed camps in their current assigned DS Division.

The reusable workspace contains Overview, Participants, Camp Stock, Distribution and History tabs. Tables support live local searching and scrolling. Forms, status colours and approval cards use the WIDMS design. Navigation and primary form labels support English, Sinhala and Tamil.

Subject Officers also have **My Camps**, filtered on the server to their own requests, with the **Conduct Camp** action after approval. **Camp History** continues to show all officers' camps, including the responsible officer, actual conducting officer from the audit record, conduct time and camp status. Viewing another officer's history never grants permission to conduct or manage their camp.

## Workflow and safeguards

- Only a Subject Officer can request a camp. It must use an active district/DS Division and the built-in Spectacles aid definition. Contact lenses are never part of this ledger.
- Admin reviews the camp; rejection requires a reason. Only its requesting Subject Officer receives that decision.
- Camp requests appear as cards under **Pending Approvals → Vision Camp Requests**, with an accurate pending count, Approve/Reject buttons and a required rejection reason. The dashboard and new request notifications link to this queue. The legacy Camp Approvals URL also opens this queue; reviewed camps remain in Camp History.
- The owner marks an approved camp conducted on/after its scheduled date, then records participants. Participant geography is fixed to the camp district/DS Division; GN choices belong to that division. NIC or Elder Card is mandatory; phone is optional. Both identification fields may be supplied. Duplicate identifiers within a camp are rejected.
- The owner's **Conduct Camp** button appears directly in Camp History and at the top of camp details after approval. It is disabled until the scheduled date, with that date displayed. Confirmation opens the separate **Camp Beneficiary Registration** page; it cannot be repeated or performed by another officer. Owners can reopen it using **Register Participant**.
- The registration page and the owner's editable Participants tab contain only one compact Aid Request-style form, without a participant table. Name, gender, address, NIC and/or Elder Card, optional phone, fixed camp district/DS Division and required GN Division are captured with the selection decision. Rejected participants require a reason. Details and decision save atomically and display the standard success notification. Previously registered, undecided participants can be selected in that form and reviewed without creating another record. Participant tables remain available in Distribution and History and for read-only viewers.
- Vision Camps use quantities only: there are no power inputs, power columns or power-matching requirements. `migration_camp_quantity_only.sql` removes the old approved-participant power constraint through the migration runner. Historical power columns/values remain intact for compatibility; the ledger combines all existing camp stock into one pool and new stock entries use a neutral compatibility key, not a prescription. Ordinary aid and contact-lens workflows are unchanged.
- Gender is installed through `migration_camp_participant_gender.sql`; old rows retain NULL rather than inventing personal data.
- Camp selection and distribution enforce the existing system Spectacles rule's `restriction_months`, using matching NIC or Elder Card history from both camp and ordinary spectacle distributions. Zero means no configured waiting period; the form says so. A blocked selection shows the last issue and next eligible dates. A rejected record can still be saved with its reason. This does not change ordinary aid's existing eligibility checks or invent a new duration.
- Store Keeper selects a conducted, approved camp in the shared **Receive Aid** form and enters the supplier, delivered quantity, whole bulk price, invoice, date and payment details. The form stores a linked supplier invoice batch and camp stock receipt in one transaction. Camp stock stays outside ordinary Central Stock. The shared Receipt History table and each camp's stock table show the supplier, cost, paid amount, balance and payment history. Older camp receipts without supplier information remain visible.
- Camp approval alone does not make the camp visible to Store Keepers or notify them. Their lists and direct detail URLs allow access only after the owner marks the approved camp conducted, when the stock-receiving workflow begins. Completed conducted camps remain available for stock history.
- The camp owner requests an available quantity and planned distribution date. Pending requests reserve availability for further requests. Admin may approve a quantity no greater than requested or reject with a reason. Approved requests automatically enter the Store Keeper's Camp Release Requests page.
- Release goes only to the camp's requesting Subject Officer. The Distribution table has separate date/remarks controls and a **Distribute** button for each approved, undistributed participant. Buttons remain disabled until stock is actually released to the owner or transferred to that specific SSO, and disable again when stock is exhausted. Store receipts and approval alone do not enable them. Server-side checks enforce this even for forged submissions. One pair is distributed per participant. Distribution dates cannot precede receipt of stock or be in the future.
- Remaining stock may be transferred only to an active SSO assigned to that camp division. The SSO can distribute only stock actually transferred to them, to approved undistributed participants in the same camp. All follow-up stays linked to the camp.
- Completion requires participant decisions and all store/Subject Officer stock to be accounted for. SSO follow-up may remain after the owner's camp completion.
- All mutations are transactional and serialize on the camp row. Submission tokens, unique distribution/identifier/reference constraints, state checks and balance checks protect against duplicate actions, over-release and overspending stock. The event ledger records actor, action and time; WIDMS activity history is updated in the same transaction.

## System and phone notifications

Camp requests go to active Admin users; decisions and stock availability/releases go only to the owning Subject Officer. Conducted camps and approved stock requests notify active Store Keepers. Transfers notify the selected division SSO; that SSO's later distributions notify the camp owner. Completion notifies Admin.

Each system notification has a matching deduplicated SMS job on the recipient's registered account phone. Jobs commit with the action, never send within its transaction, and use the existing post-submission/scheduled worker. Participant phone numbers are not recipients of staff workflow messages. SMS omits participant names and prescriptions. Delivery depends on the configured gateway and worker being available.

## Checks

`php tests/spectacle-camps.php --render` exercises the full workflow and all role/tab layouts using connection-local temporary schema clones and fake SMS senders. It never changes operational records or sends real messages. It tests ownership, division boundaries, approval gates, rejection reasons, duplicate submissions, quantity-only balances including legacy stock, release-gated row actions, transfers, translations, history and notification recipients. `--preview` also creates a static fixture HTML preview, not live data.
